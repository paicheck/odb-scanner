Range, distance and consumption

Four different things live on this page and are kept apart: estimated remaining range (a prediction the vehicle makes), distance actually travelled (odometer readings), state of charge, and consumption figures the vehicle stored. A range estimate is not measured distance and not a measure of battery health.
